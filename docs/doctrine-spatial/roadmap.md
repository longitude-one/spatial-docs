description: Roadmap of the longitude-one/doctrine-spatial library

This roadmap shows announced support dates and release forecasts for doctrine-spatial.

A major release may increase the minimum supported PHP version. Other breaking changes are documented in the changelog.

Dates and Composer constraints come from the [shared roadmap configuration](../shared/support-timeline-generation.md#maintaining-library-roadmaps).
Support dates retain the existing roadmap commitments. Monthly release forecasts remain approximate until an exact date is announced.
Actual initial release dates are verified against the [published releases](https://github.com/longitude-one/doctrine-spatial/releases).
