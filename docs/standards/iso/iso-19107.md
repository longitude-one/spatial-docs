---
title: ISO 19107 — Spatial schema
description: Records the ISO spatial-schema sources, LongitudeOne geometry-model usage and the limits of the available normative evidence.
---

ISO 19107 describes conceptual models for vector geometry and topology, together
with spatial operations for geographic information. Its subjects include geometric
objects, their dimensions and relationships; it is broader than a catalogue of
PHP classes or an interchange format. This scope is identified by the official
[ISO 2003][iso2003] and [ISO 2019][iso2019] abstracts.

LongitudeOne uses geometry-family and dimension classifications in its PHP model.
The implementation evidence below explains how those classifications are checked;
it does not establish compliance with an unverified ISO edition.

## Normative sources and edition scope

| Published standard | Edition and publication | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISO 19107:2003 | Edition 1, May 2003 | [ISO catalogue][iso2003] |
| ISO 19107:2019 | Edition 2, December 2019 | [ISO catalogue][iso2019] |

The title of both editions is *Geographic information — Spatial schema*.

LongitudeOne does not have the budget to acquire the published ISO standards.
No complete ISO 19107 text or ISO 19107 draft has been verified for this reference.
Requirements, conformance classes, amendments and corrigenda remain **Not assessed**.

**Source investigation for Story #61 (2026-10-11):** the
[OGC Abstract Specification catalogue][ogc-catalogue] identifies the 2019
adoption as **19-092**, but its download links to the ISO catalogue rather than
an accessible normative text. The historical **01-101** link also points to ISO;
the legacy `docs.ogc.org/as/01-101/01-101.pdf` and
`www.opengis.net/docs/01-101.pdf` paths return 404. The catalogue's **17-087r13**
link resolves to *Features and geometry — Part 1: Feature models*, not an
equivalent ISO 19107 publication.

The accessible [CityGML conceptual model, OGC 20-010][citygml], clauses
8.1.3–8.1.10, attributes selected class definitions to ISO 19107:2003.
Those extracts can support a bounded assessment of those classes; they do not
provide the complete ISO geometry, topology, dimension and conformance clauses
needed for the intended assessment. They do not establish a project decision
to adopt that edition. Completing Task #65 therefore still requires an
applicable normative source and an established edition, or explicit Product
Owner acceptance of a reduced assessment scope.

Two available sources establish an **indirect reference path**, with separate authority:

- OGC Simple Feature Access 1.2.1, **OGC 06-103r4**, Introduction, identifies its
  model as a profile of **ISO 19107:2003**. Its Annex A describes the correspondence
  but is informative. The [official OGC document][sfa] provides that evidence;
  it does not establish adoption of ISO 19107:2019 by LongitudeOne.
- The project-held **ISO/IEC CD 13249-3:201x(E)**, dated **2009-01-16**, cites
  ISO 19107 without an edition in 2.2 and imports spatial terms in 3.1.3.
  This is a SQL/MM Committee Draft, not an ISO 19107 draft. See the
  [SQL/MM source record](iso-iec-13249-3.md#normative-source).

## How LongitudeOne uses spatial-schema concepts

### Geometry families and implementation availability

LongitudeOne identifies families with `GeometryTypeEnum`: Point, curve and
surface families, collections and additional model families. Its
`isInstantiable()` classification is checked for every enum case by
[`GeometryTypeEnumTest::testInstantiabilityClassification()`][geometry-tests].

The separate [`SpatialTypeImplementationStatus::isFullyImplemented()`][status] check
records class availability.

The [SQL/MM reference](iso-iec-13249-3.md#geometry-model-and-dimensions) records
the verified SQL/MM comparison for its geometry family.

### Geometry dimension and coordinate layouts

LongitudeOne keeps the geometry's intrinsic dimension separate from the number
of coordinate ordinates. `TopologicalDimensionEnum` distinguishes points (0),
curves (1), surfaces (2) and volumes (3), with a separate empty value (-1).
`GeometryTypeEnum::topologicalDimension()` maps families to that classification;
mixed collections have no single type-level dimension.

`CoordinateDimensionEnum` separately describes XY, XYZ, XYM and XYZM layouts.
Its tests check ordinate counts, order and Z/M presence. Consequently, an XYZM
point remains point-dimensional even though its coordinate has four ordinates.
This is **LongitudeOne implementation behavior**, not a claim that an ISO edition
requires these exact PHP enums or the empty sentinel.

`TopologicalDimensionEnumTest` checks the dimension categories, while
`GeometryTypeEnumTest` checks their family mapping. These tests establish the
expected PHP classification. Direct ISO verification would additionally need
the selected edition's definitions and applicable object constraints.

## LongitudeOne support and assessment limits

**Core** denotes Spatial Core **1.3.0**, revision
`1289582288e8954243de5ccb1f28ab7e78f4e9a4`.
**Types** denotes the Spatial Types **pre-1.0 development snapshot** after
`0.0.1-alpha.4`, revision `ceaee5d142c77fc3922ab50c762fa6648fea0e30`.
Snapshot findings do not promise released availability. No DBMS scope is assessed.

The supported records describe PHP features only. Their equivalence to individual
ISO 19107 requirements remains **Not assessed**.

| Feature | Implementation scope | Status | Limitations | Implementation decision | Extensions | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family classification | Core | Supported | PHP metadata only | Explicit enum cases | Not assessed | [Enum][geometry]; [tests][geometry-tests] |
| Dimension distinction | Core | Supported | PHP primitives only | Separate enums | Not assessed | [Topology tests][topology-tests]; [layout tests][layout-tests] |
| Geometry classes | Types | Partially supported | Subset below | PHP objects | Not assessed | [Types catalogue][types] |
| ISO conformance | Core and Types | Not assessed | ISO text unavailable | No blanket claim | Not assessed | No direct ISO audit |

The [Types catalogue][types] documents Point,
LineString, Polygon, CircularString, CompoundCurve, Triangle, GeometryCollection,
MultiPoint, MultiLineString and MultiPolygon in XY/XYZ/XYM/XYZM, and
PolyhedralSurface in XYZ/XYZM. It also identifies unavailable families, including
CurvePolygon, MultiCurve, MultiSurface, TIN and BRepSolid. These are implementation
gaps in the assessed snapshot, not an inventory of all ISO 19107 requirements.

No LongitudeOne extension to a specific ISO 19107 edition is asserted: deciding
whether a model feature exceeds that edition requires its verified text.
The missing edition-specific source prevents a complete normative assessment.
